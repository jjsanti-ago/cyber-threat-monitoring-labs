# Zendesk — Ticketing & Incident Documentation

## Overview

Zendesk is a ticketing and customer support platform used to manage, document, track, and resolve support requests.

Zendesk was included in my Cyber Threat Monitoring Level I training as part of the Ticketing Management System activity.

## Objectives

- Understand the purpose of a ticketing system
- Create and document support tickets
- Organize and track reported issues
- Practice structured technical documentation
- Understand basic ticket lifecycle and escalation concepts

## Ticket Management Concepts

A typical ticket workflow may include:

1. Receive the reported issue
2. Create and document the ticket
3. Categorize the request
4. Determine priority
5. Investigate and document relevant information
6. Provide or record the resolution
7. Escalate when necessary
8. Update the ticket
9. Close the ticket after resolution

## Skills & Knowledge

- Ticket documentation
- Issue categorization
- Incident tracking
- Technical documentation
- Basic escalation concepts
- Communication and collaboration

## Cybersecurity Relevance

Ticketing systems help security and IT teams document incidents, technical issues, investigations, and actions taken.

Structured documentation provides a record that can support troubleshooting, escalation, incident response, and organizational accountability.

## IT Support Relevance

Ticketing knowledge is also applicable to IT Helpdesk and Service Desk roles where support requests must be properly documented, prioritized, monitored, and resolved within defined procedures.
