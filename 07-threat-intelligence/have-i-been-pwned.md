# Have I Been Pwned

## Overview

Have I Been Pwned is a service that provides information about whether an email address has appeared in known data breaches.

This resource was covered as part of my Cyber Threat Monitoring Level I training under Threat Intelligence.

## Objectives

- Understand the concept of data breaches
- Learn how breach exposure can be assessed
- Understand the security risks associated with compromised accounts
- Familiarize myself with defensive threat intelligence resources

## Skills & Knowledge

- Breach awareness
- Account exposure assessment
- Threat intelligence research
- Defensive security awareness
- Security risk identification

## Cybersecurity Relevance

Information about data breaches can help individuals and organizations understand potential account exposure and improve security controls.

In a security operations context, breach intelligence can provide additional context when assessing compromised credentials or potential account-related threats.

## Responsible Use

Only authorized email addresses and appropriate information should be checked.

Sensitive information, credentials, or personal data obtained from breaches should never be collected, shared, or misused.
